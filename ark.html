Questions.

Everything you need to know about releasing music with 444Music.

Log in to your dashboard, create a new release, upload your audio file and cover art, and fill in the details such as artist name, release date and genre.

Your music can be delivered to major platforms including Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, YouTube Music, Audiomack, Boomplay and more, over 100 stores in total.

Most releases are processed within 2–5 business days. We recommend submitting at least 7 days before your target release date.

We recommend WAV files (16-bit or 24-bit) for best quality. FLAC is also accepted. We convert to the format each store needs.

You earn royalties whenever people stream or download your music on supported platforms. Earnings show in your dashboard, and you keep 100% of what's yours.

Streaming platforms usually report earnings monthly, but some may take 2–3 months before the first royalties appear in your dashboard.
