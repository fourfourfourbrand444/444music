Earnings Manager
Total earnings are auto-calculated from the splits collection, plus directly from your submissions for any release that has no splits doc at all — whether that's genuinely solo, or a collab release whose split doc never got created. To pay out a release, enter an amount under that song and hit Distribute — releases with collaborators divide by each person's locked %, solo releases credit the owner in full. Every distribute action also updates that artist's users/{uid}.earnings field, so the mobile app and website earnings pages reflect it automatically. Every card also has a Manual earnings override — always available, no release or split required — that directly sets the live earnings field to any value, up or down, for correcting mistakes or paying artists with nothing on file yet.
