Who we are

444Music Distribution is an independent artist platform that connects emerging artists with listeners around the world. We give you the tools major labels have, without the gatekeeping.

Our mission is simple: put artists in control of their careers, their royalties and their fans.

What you get

Fast deliveryYour music reaches Spotify, Apple Music and more within 24 to 48 hours.
AnalyticsFollow your streams and earnings across platforms in one place.
Rights protectionISRC registration is included with every release.
SupportOur team is available seven days a week.
100% of your royaltiesWe take nothing from your earnings.

Information we collect

Account detailsYour name, email address and artist profile.
PaymentsHandled by secure third-party providers. We never store your card details.
UsageBasic analytics that help us improve the platform.
Music detailsTitle, ISRC, release date and genre, so we can distribute your release correctly.

We never sell or rent your data, and we never share it for advertising.

Your rights

AccessRequest a copy of your personal data at any time from your settings.
CorrectionUpdate inaccurate information from your profile.
DeletionClose your account and ask us to delete your personal data within 30 days.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the platform and optional analytics cookies to improve it. You can manage cookies in your browser settings. We do not use advertising trackers.

Content rules

OwnershipYou must own the copyright or hold exclusive distribution rights to everything you upload.
Not allowedUnlicensed samples or covers, AI-generated music presented as original, hate speech, graphic violence and illegal material.
Accurate detailsEvery release needs the correct artist name, title, genre and release date.

Royalties and payouts

Royalty rate100% of net royalties go to the artist.
Minimum payoutYour balance must reach $10 USD before you can withdraw.
Processing time3 to 7 working days after you request a withdrawal.
Payment methodsBank transfer, PayPal and Mobile Money in selected regions.
Reporting delayPlatforms usually report earnings 45 to 60 days after the month ends, so new earnings can take up to 60 days to appear.

Suspension and closing your account

Artificial streamsBots, stream farms or any fake engagement lead to a permanent ban and loss of withheld royalties.
Copyright violationsRepeated infringement leads to suspension and takedown requests to all platforms.
Closing your accountYou can close it at any time. Pending royalties are paid after verification.
Changes to these termsWe will email registered artists 30 days before any update.
