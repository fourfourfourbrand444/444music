Release your music to the world from your phone. 444Music is a distribution platform built for independent artists. Upload your songs and cover art, submit your release, and we take it to major music stores and streaming platforms, so your fans can find you wherever they listen.

Keep every dollar. There are no hidden fees. 100% of your royalties go straight to you, every time.

Unlimited releases. Distribute as many releases as you want, whenever you are ready.

Follow every release. See where each release stands, from draft to in review to approved, without chasing anyone for an update.

Know your numbers. Check your total streams, how your streaming has grown over time, and which stores your streams come from.

Get paid in the app. Watch your balance, see what each song has earned, and withdraw your earnings when you reach the minimum.

Stay connected. Share updates with stories and posts, and follow other artists on 444Music.

Made for independent artists everywhere. Questions? Write to us at 444musicdistro@gmail.com.

How to install
  1. Tap Download and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the file. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, just this once.
  3. Tap Install, then open 444Music.

Updating later? Download again and install over the old version. Your account stays as it is.
